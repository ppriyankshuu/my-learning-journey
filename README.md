## 📂 Featured Projects & Production Pipelines

### 1. 🏆 Weekly Automated Business Report Pipeline (Capstone)
* **File:** [`capstone_pipeline.py`](capstone_pipeline.py)
* **Stack:** Python, Pandas, SQLite, Relational SQL
* **Business Problem:** Ingest dirty weekly Monday sales logs, clean text formatting, store in a relational SQL database, and automatically generate executive performance reports.
* **Key Strategic Insight:** While all sales reps closed an identical 5 deals each, sales rep **Ravi generated ₹249,000 (nearly 3x higher revenue)** due to a strategic focus on high-ticket Electronics over low-margin Groceries.
* **Deliverable:** Automated export of [`weekly_executive_summary.csv`](weekly_executive_summary.csv) in <2 seconds.

### 2. 💳 E-Commerce Transaction Analysis & VIP Segmentation
* **File:** [`project2_ecommerce.py`](project2_ecommerce.py)
* **Stack:** Python, Pandas, Boolean Filtering
* **Business Problem:** Analyze payment channel efficiency across Indian metros and isolate high-value customer orders.
* **Key Strategic Insight:** **UPI emerged as the #1 revenue driver (₹61,780)**, outperforming Credit Cards and Cash on Delivery. 100% of orders over ₹10,000 belonged to the Electronics vertical.
* **Deliverable:** Automated generation of [`vip_customers_report.csv`](vip_customers_report.csv).

### 5. 📈 Interactive E-Commerce Sales BI Dashboard
* **File:** [`ecommerce_analytics_dashboard.pbix`](ecommerce_analytics_dashboard.pbix)
* **Stack:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop (Interactive Slicers, KPI Cards, Clustered Bar Visuals, Donut Charts)
* **Business Problem:** Build an executive-facing dynamic dashboard that tracks multi-city transactional performance across product categories and payment channels.
* **Key Strategic Insight:** UPI and Credit Card drive over 92% of all digital order volume. Implementing category-level cross-filtering reveals Electronics as the dominant margin contributor across Bangalore and Hyderabad.
* **Deliverable:** Live interactive `.pbix` report with cross-filtered category slicing and instant KPI aggregations.
